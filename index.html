About Me

Hi, I'm Xiaoyin

I come from an AI background — but it was my own body that changed everything. Years of chronic pain pushed me to study movement science, understand biomechanics, and figure out why I was hurting.

I trained myself back to full strength. No more pain. Along the way, I helped friends do the same — and realized this was what I wanted to do for more people.

Today I hold an ACE Personal Trainer certification and a NASM Corrective Exercise Specialization. I bring both the analytical mindset of someone who studied AI and the lived experience of someone who has been through it — to help you build a stronger, pain-free body.

My Approach

The Shine Method

Four principles that guide every session, every program, every client.

S
Science First
Every exercise choice is backed by biomechanics, exercise physiology, and neuromuscular science. No trends, no gimmicks — just what actually works.
H
Heal to Perform
Pain and old injuries shouldn't hold you back forever. We address the root cause, rebuild strength around it, and get you moving freely again.
I
Intelligent Training
AI-assisted programming that adapts to your body's data — so your plan evolves as you do, not the other way around.
N
Nutrition-Informed
Training and nutrition work together, not in isolation. Every program considers how you fuel your body to support recovery, performance, and long-term results.
E
Empower to Be Independent
My goal isn't to keep you dependent on a trainer forever — it's to give you the knowledge and tools to train confidently on your own.
